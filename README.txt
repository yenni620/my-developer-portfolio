THE LITTLE CODE ATELIER
=======================

Open index.html in a browser to view the portfolio. Keep index.html, style.css,
script.js, and the images folder together so relative paths continue to work.

YOUR PHOTOS
-----------
The home portrait is at images/profile.png and uses the supplied lace template
at images/decorations/profile-lace-frame.jpg. The About portrait is at
images/about-me.png, and the school photo is at images/school.png. Replace any
of these files while keeping its filename, or update its image src in index.html.

The project images are images/project-1.png, images/project-2-login.png, and
images/project-2-dashboard.png.

The Education section keeps its story format and also includes an accessible
academic summary table for the lab requirement.

Add any personal or decorative images under images/ or images/decorations/.
The current floral, paper, frame, lace, and sticker details are drawn with CSS
and text characters. Small floating glitter and shimmer particles animate along
the page edges; they stay outside the reading area, ignore clicks, and pause for
visitors who prefer reduced motion.

EDITING CONTENT
---------------
Edit index.html to change words, profile links, project details, or placeholders.
The TikTok profile link is omitted. Project 01 has no details link, and Project
02 links to the supplied live portal. Update colors, layout, and type in style.css.

The contact form opens a prefilled email draft in the visitor's email app; it
does not send a message automatically. The direct email link is also available.
Google Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts when online; local fallback fonts are
included for offline use. The hero greeting uses Ballet; the tagline and About
greeting use Allura and Italianno.
The small click chime is generated in the browser and can be switched off with
the Sound button in the header. The About portrait is revealed by its button and
plays a separate short sparkle chime when opened.
